**Context:** wer-kennt-wen.de was one of Germany's largest social networks with 9+ million members,
acquired by RTL interactive in 2009 and shut down in June 2014. I joined as PHP developer, led
development from 2008 to 2010 and then returned to backend development.

## Role & Contributions

- Moved the codebase from procedural code to an object-oriented, multi-layered MVC architecture;
  migrated the group and messaging systems to Zend Framework.
- Built the REST API (described in WADL) and the HTML mobile portal.
- Scaled MySQL with vertical and horizontal partitioning and extensive Memcache use; tuned queries
  with EXPLAIN and profiled with Xdebug.
- Security: brute-force protection, XSS, CSRF and SQL-injection prevention, ACLs.
- Introduced Jenkins CI (later also for Java projects), PHPUnit with coverage analysis and Selenium
  tests; implemented Sphinx search.

### Head of Development (Oct 2008 — Mar 2010)

- Change-request management, roadmap and release management.
- Software architecture with UML; new workflows, procedures and interface definitions.
- Code reviews and software metrics (StatSVN, phploc); platform load tests.
- Personnel planning, hiring, onboarding and performance reviews.

## Results

- An architecture that scaled to 9+ million registered users.
- Page load times down 60% through the architecture modernization.
- [Final employment reference](https://github.com/SchulteDev/SchulteDev/blob/main/_data/credentials/wer-kennt-wen/2014_wkw_Zeugnis.pdf) (German).

## Takeaways

- I took the Head of Development role without management experience, assuming technical skill
  would carry it. It didn't: leadership is mostly about people. With training and reflection I found
  a hands-off leadership style that suits both me and the team.
- I chose Sphinx over ElasticSearch because I knew it. The platform later migrated to ElasticSearch;
  familiarity is a weak reason for a technology choice.
