\cvsection{Working Principles}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Self-contained Systems over microservices:} At Trusted Shops simple changes spread across many services. With SCS, autonomy plus automation delivered both throughput and quality.
\item \textbf{Fit-for-purpose platforms:} I see Kubernetes as a tool for IT operations providers. Simple applications are better served by managed hyperscaler services.
\item \textbf{You build it, you run it:} Every handover to a separate operations team costs communication effort.
\item \textbf{Agents need good engineering:} Even with the best models, it is ``garbage in, garbage out''. My throughput rose enormously, but judging code reliably remains the prerequisite.
\item \textbf{Analysis over familiarity:} Choosing Sphinx over ElasticSearch out of familiarity proved suboptimal; colleagues migrated the platform to ElasticSearch in 2012.
\item \textbf{Leadership is its own skill:} I took on Head of Development without management experience. That taught me the human factor and led me to a laissez-faire leadership style.
\end{itemize}
