\cvsection{Lessons Learned}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Quality and paid-down debt pay off:} Most of my measured 2026 results come from paying down technical debt. As sole developer at Saloodo!, tests and automation quickly paid for themselves.
\item \textbf{Self-contained Systems over microservices:} At Union Investment, team autonomy plus automation kept both throughput and quality high. At Trusted Shops, simple changes spanned many microservices.
\item \textbf{Simple beats complex:} A plain pub/sub on Azure Event Hubs solved a search architecture that first seemed impossible. An event-driven AWS Lambda integration would have made the Saloodo! middleware cheaper and more robust.
\item \textbf{Decide once validated:} The 60 saved person-days came from implementing Web Components integration right after a successful PoC. I did not explore every alternative first.
\item \textbf{You build it, you run it:} Handovers to separate operations teams cost communication effort. Simple applications are better served by managed services than by Kubernetes.
\item \textbf{Leadership is its own craft:} Becoming Head of Development without management experience taught me the human factor. I found a laissez-faire style that suits me and the team. Choosing Sphinx over ElasticSearch out of familiarity taught me not to let comfort decide.
\end{itemize}
