\cvsection{Working Principles \& Lessons Learned}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Simple and proven beats complex:} a plain publish/subscribe design on Azure Event Hubs solved a search architecture that first seemed impossible. In hindsight, eventing with AWS Lambda would have made a separate CRM middleware unnecessary.
\item \textbf{Quality investment accelerates delivery:} as a sole developer, rigorous tests and automation quickly paid for themselves. Paying down technical debt produced most of my measured results at Union Investment.
\item \textbf{You build it, you run it:} every handover to a separate operations team costs communication effort and brings friction between working styles.
\item \textbf{Decide on usage, not familiarity:} choosing Sphinx over ElasticSearch out of familiarity proved suboptimal; colleagues later migrated the platform to ElasticSearch.
\item \textbf{Leadership is about people:} taking on Head of Development without management experience taught me the human factor. I evolved toward a laissez-faire style that suits me and the team.
\item \textbf{Agents need good engineering:} even with the best models it is ``garbage in, garbage out''. A codebase needs good developer and agent experience to stay maintainable.
\end{itemize}
