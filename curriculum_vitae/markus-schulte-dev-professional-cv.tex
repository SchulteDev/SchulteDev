\section*{Lessons Learned}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Paying down tech debt pays off:} most of my measured 2026 results came from it, and the team profits.
  \item \textbf{Self-contained Systems:} team autonomy plus automation kept both throughput and quality high.
  \item \textbf{You build it, you run it:} every handover to separate operations teams costs communication effort.
  \item \textbf{Agents need good engineering:} even with the best models it is still ``garbage in, garbage out''.
\end{itemize}
