\section*{Working Principles}
\secrule
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Minimal effort, established best practices, small tasks:} reuse what exists; cut architecture into small stories that each reach production on their own.
  \item \textbf{You build it, you run it:} every handover to separate operations teams costs communication effort.
  \item \textbf{Tech debt pays off:} most of the measured 2026 improvements came from paying down technical debt.
  \item \textbf{Simple beats clever:} Event Hubs pub/sub for search, SCS instead of microservices by default, managed services instead of self-run Kubernetes for simple applications.
  \item \textbf{Agents need good engineering:} even with the best models it is ``garbage in, garbage out'' -- quality gates and a good developer and agent experience remain essential.
\end{itemize}
