\section*{Summary: The Unpolished Version}
Software Engineer \& Cloud Architect with 19+ years of hands-on experience (12+ as a freelancer) in enterprise
modernization and cloud-native transformations \textendash{} and a matching record of things that did not go to plan.
Regular CVs list the 9M+ users, the 99.9\% uptime and the deployments cut from days to minutes. This one also lists
what it took: a Head of Development role accepted without management experience, a search engine chosen for
familiarity, a middleware that in hindsight should not have existed, and two encounters (microservices, Kubernetes)
that turned me into a skeptic. Today I work agent-first: coding agents write most of the code, I keep architecture,
scope and quality decisions \textendash{} and I have learned that even with the best models it is still ``garbage in,
garbage out''. Currently IT architect at Union Investment and, privately, running BitterQuant, an ML trading research
project that has already produced a respectable collection of well-documented null results.

\section*{Key Impacts and Their Fine Print}
\begin{itemize}
\item Urgent deployments cut from several days to 30 minutes and 99.9\% uptime at Union Investment \textendash{} after first
  learning at Trusted Shops what microservices feel like without tests and automation.
\item Saved \textasciitilde 60 person-days by building on Web Components right after the PoC instead of exploring every
  other integration path \textendash{} sometimes a validated decision beats a complete option analysis.
\item 100\% production uptime over three years for a Golang middleware syncing \textasciitilde 10 million records to
  Salesforce \textendash{} a component that, in hindsight, was architecturally unnecessary.
\item Backend line coverage 34\% \textrightarrow{} 87\% \textendash{} achieved by fixing the measurement, not by writing tests.
  The 3\texttimes{} unit tests in the calculator monorepo (538 \textrightarrow{} 1,659) were actual work, though.
\item Renovate PRs merged \textasciitilde 9\texttimes{} faster (median \textasciitilde 2\,h \textrightarrow{} 13\,min) in a
  monorepo where Renovate had been intermittently broken for months. Dependency hygiene beats dependency archaeology.
\item 1,065 hidden type errors uncovered and fixed to zero in a sibling team's monorepo \textendash{} TypeScript had been
  politely keeping them to itself until a type-check gate asked.
\item Scaled a social network to 9+ million members (partitioning, Memcache) and cut page load times by 60\%
  \textendash{} the company still ceased operations in 2014.
\item 83 customers in 15+ countries for my Bamboo add-ons over seven years \textendash{} a first-mover advantage in a niche.
\end{itemize}
