\cvsection{The Other Half of My CV}
My regular CV lists platforms for 9M+ users, 99.9\% uptime and deployment times cut from days to minutes. This one covers the rest: a leadership role accepted too early, a search engine chosen out of habit, a middleware that should never have been built, a microservices landscape that made me skeptical of microservices, and a first encounter with Kubernetes that made me a skeptic to this day. I am a Software Engineer and Cloud Architect with 19+ years of hands-on experience, 12+ of them freelance, currently back at Union Investment as IT architect for three teams. In parallel I run BitterQuant, a private ML research project on systematic trading -- paper trading since May 2026, with the emphasis on \emph{paper}. Since 2025 coding agents write most of my code, so I now review a machine's mistakes in addition to my own. Every failure below came with a lesson -- usually right after the failure, which is how lessons tend to work.
