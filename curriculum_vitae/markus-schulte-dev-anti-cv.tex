\section*{CV Summary: The Honest Version}
Software engineer and cloud architect with 19+ years of hands-on experience (12+ as a freelancer).
Java is my mother tongue (19+ years), Golang my cloud-native favorite (5+ years), and enterprise
modernization my daily bread. Since April 2026 I am back at Union Investment as IT architect for
three teams of the funds squad, hands-on in the team behind the public financial calculators. In
parallel I run BitterQuant, a private ML research project on systematic trading on AWS. I work
agent-first: coding agents write most of the code, and I keep the architecture, scope and quality
decisions.

\vspace{3pt}
My slogan is ``Crafting solutions that thrive today and tomorrow''. This document covers the
yesterday part: wrong technology picks, a leadership role I was not ready for, and two technologies
I have been skeptical of for years while using both professionally. Read it as the footnotes to the
regular CV: every win below comes with its catch.
