\section*{Anti-CV Summary}
Software engineer and cloud architect with 19+ years of hands-on experience (12+ as a freelancer), specializing in enterprise modernization and cloud-native transformations. This is the ordinary CV with the footnotes promoted to main text: the technology calls I got wrong, the leadership lessons I learned the hard way, and the systems that turned out to be unnecessary. Since April 2026 I am back at Union Investment as IT architect for three teams of the funds squad, hands-on in the team building the public financial calculators. In parallel I run BitterQuant, a private ML research project on systematic trading, where two promising approaches collapsed once execution was simulated realistically. I work agent-first: coding agents write most of the code, I keep architecture, scope and quality decisions and, regrettably, the responsibility. Even with the best models it remains garbage in, garbage out.
